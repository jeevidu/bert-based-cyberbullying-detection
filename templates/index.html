How the App Works.

Log In

To log in, go to the Login page, enter your registered email and password, then click the Login button. You’ll be instantly directed to the homepage where you can start analyzing text for cyberbullying.

Create

To create an account, visit the Registration page and fill in your name, mobile number, date of birth, email, and a secure password. After clicking the Submit button, your account will be created, and you’ll be ready to log in and use the app.

Send

To send a message, type or paste your message into the text box, and click the Send button. The system will instantly analyze the message and show whether it contains cyberbullying content.

The Best Features of The App.

Text & Image

The app’s key feature is real-time cyberbullying detection, powered by advanced BERT-based models. It instantly analyzes user input for harmful language, providing immediate feedback through warning messages and a protective shield icon, ensuring a safe and secure online experience for all users.

The app’s Public Chatting feature allows users to interact freely in an open environment, while ensuring safety with automated monitoring. It detects inappropriate content in real-time, giving users the freedom to communicate without compromising security or comfort.
